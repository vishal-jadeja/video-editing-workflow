# Audio provenance

All sound effects are original procedural synthesis in `scripts/sfx.py`: filtered noise whooshes, decaying sine ticks, a two-tone error blip and a three-note positive chime. No downloaded SFX, samples, loops or music. No background music is used.

The initial export uses Apple's installed Daniel system voice, expressly approved by the user as an alternative to OpenAI TTS. No external voice cloning or recorded human performance is used. OpenAI mode uses `gpt-4o-mini-tts` with `cedar`, authenticated only through `OPENAI_API_KEY`. Narration is synthetic; disclose this in the upload description (suggested: “AI-generated narration”).

Fonts: Inter and JetBrains Mono, downloaded from Google Fonts by `scripts/prepare.mjs`, licensed under the SIL Open Font License. https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter and https://fonts.google.com/specimen/JetBrains+Mono
